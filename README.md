# 🕊️ The Seeker: Path of the Wayfarer
### *A 3D Stylized Biblical Adventure (Inspired by Mini Ninjas)*

A vibrant third-person 3D action-exploration game set in ancient biblical Judea. Play as **Eli the Seeker**, an agile traveler equipped with a shepherd's staff, traveling pouch, and a heart seeking wisdom. Explore ancient villages, aid residents in quests rooted in biblical scripture, and discover sacred scrolls for your chronicle.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Mini-Ninjas Inspired 3D Movement**:
  - Energetic run cycle with dynamic forward lean and dust puff effects.
  - Fluid jump & vault animations with landing compression.
  - Quick dodge roll ([R]) for speed and evasive maneuvers.
  - Orbiting third-person chase camera with mouse drag and zoom controls.
- **Spiritual Abilities**:
  - **Light of Faith ([Q])**: Channels prayer through your shepherd's crook staff, illuminating ancient caverns and glowing Hebrew runes.
- **Biblically Grounded Quests**:
  1. **The Parable of the Lost Sheep (Luke 15:4-7)**: Search the rocky hills for 3 wandering lambs, comfort them, and guide them back into the stone sheepfold. Unlocks the *Scroll of the Good Shepherd (Psalm 23)*.
  2. **Living Water at the Well (John 4:13-14)**: Climb the eastern Watchtower Ruins, recover the lost stone crank gear, and restore the village well to bring refreshing water to Bethany. Unlocks the *Scroll of Living Water*.
  3. **The Five Smooth Stones of Elah (1 Samuel 17:40)**: Search along the dry brook bed for the five radiant stones of faith (Faith, Courage, Truth, Peace, Righteousness). Unlocks the *Scroll of David's Courage*.
  4. **Light in the Cavern (Isaiah 9:2 & Psalm 119:105)**: Venture into the western cavern and cast the Light of Faith to unlock the Ark of Remembrance.
- **Scripture Journal (Parchment Codex)**:
  - Accessible at any time ([Tab]).
  - Displays all unlocked biblical scrolls with full verses, references, and spiritual reflections.
- **Built-in Procedural Audio Synthesizer**:
  - Ancient Middle Eastern melodic lyre/harp arpeggios, gentle wind ambience, footsteps, adorable sheep bleats, and glorious quest fanfares using 100% Web Audio API (zero external broken audio assets).
